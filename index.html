Tee Time Bot — Settings

Days to book

The bot fires at midnight on each of these days and books that same weekday 7 days out (when it releases).

Wake time

When the Mac wakes the night before, so it's ready to book at midnight (when tee times release). Late evening only (22:00–23:59).

Ideal tee times

Books the exact time, or the nearest open foursome within 2h. Each round after the first is placed ≥3h after it (ideally the same gap you set here).
